Home glute sessions 🏋🏽‍♀️

Choose a day. Start with two hard glute days and make Saturday optional. Use a loaded backpack, dumbbells, or bands if available.

8-week progression

  • Weeks 1–2: establish comfortable loads, leave 2–3 reps in reserve.
  • Weeks 3–4: add reps before weight, without sacrificing form.
  • Weeks 5–6: gradually increase resistance if recovery is good.
  • Weeks 7–8: keep progressing; reduce volume if fatigue builds.

Rest 2–3 minutes for demanding sets; 60–90 seconds for accessories. Avoid deliberately arching your lower back during bridges or thrusts.
